Basma - free Android logo maker

Basma is a simple Android app for making a logo for your business in minutes, with no design experience. Type your business name in Arabic or English, pick a symbol, font and colors, and export PNG or SVG.

Download APK (v1.5.3)

In development · قيد التطوير · version 1.5.3

  • 80+ symbols, 12 Arabic and Latin fonts
  • 11 layouts and 5 styles including metallic gold
  • Templates, palettes and custom colors
  • Move, resize and rotate each element, with undo and redo
  • Export PNG (white or transparent) or SVG

Android 7.0 or later. Logos are built from in-app templates, not by AI. No account, no ads. Not from the Play Store, so Play Protect may warn about an unknown developer.
